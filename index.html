XIANG Hanyi is currently a student of The Chinese University of Hong Kong. He holds an International Baccalaureate Diploma from Shanghai World Foreign Language Academy. His major department is Physics and streams to Astronomy and Astrophysics. He is also taking courses under the Department of Chinese Language and Literature.

In Astronomy, he is especially interested in the field of cosmic observation. Beyond that, he is interested in different styles of expressions within words and texts which drive his mind to the realm of Literature. For him, Astronomy and Literature are intertwined with each other through several symbolic figures of romanticism, and somehow, the power of imagination depicts them together.

In his spare time, music is another companion. He enjoys the freedom provided by the various compositions of harmonies and tries to taste music lyrics as a form of Literature. By treating lyrics as a distinct literary genre—both related to and in contrast with poetry—he acquires a broader and more vivid perspective in his literary studies.
